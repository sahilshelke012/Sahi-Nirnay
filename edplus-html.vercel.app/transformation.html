Six Months of Transformation. One Extraordinary You.

A personalised one-to-one coaching journey for young adults who want to think independently and make responsible decisions about wealth, family and leadership.

Overview

Core Development Areas

  • Self-awareness
  • Wealth understanding
  • Financial judgement
  • Responsibility
  • Family decisions
  • Leadership and legacy

Five Stages

  • Awareness Understand yourself and your relationship with wealth.
  • Understanding Explore wealth structures and opportunities.
  • Judgement Evaluate decisions rationally.
  • Responsibility Act with accountability.
  • Leadership Establish purpose and legacy.

Six-Month Journey

  • Month 1. My Relationship with Wealth Explore financial identity and personal beliefs through the My Wealth Story exercise.
  • Month 2. Understanding the Family Wealth Map family wealth structures, ownership and investments through the Family Wealth Map exercise.
  • Month 3. Financial Judgement Practise JUDGEMENT: Justify, Understand, Define, Gauge, Explore, Measure, Emotion, Next, Test.
  • Month 4. From Beneficiary to Steward Explore entitlement, responsibility, stewardship and long-term wealth preservation.
  • Month 5. Family, Power & Decisions Explore family business, sibling dynamics, marriage, succession and generational expectations.
  • Month 6. Leadership & Legacy Develop a Personal Wealth Philosophy based on values, purpose, responsibility and legacy.
