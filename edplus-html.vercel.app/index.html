The Transformation

Your Six-Month Journey

Month 1

My Relationship with Wealth

Explore financial identity, personal beliefs and the “My Wealth Story” exercise.

Month 2

Understanding the Family Wealth

Explore family wealth structures, ownership, investments and the “Family Wealth Map” exercise.

Month 3

Financial Judgement

Apply JUDGEMENT: Justify, Understand, Define, Gauge, Explore, Measure, Emotion, Next, Test.

Month 4

From Beneficiary to Steward

Explore entitlement, responsibility, stewardship and long-term wealth preservation.

Month 5

Family, Power & Decisions

Explore family business decisions, sibling dynamics, marriage, succession and generational expectations.

Month 6

Leadership & Legacy

Develop a Personal Wealth Philosophy based on values, purpose, responsibility and legacy.
