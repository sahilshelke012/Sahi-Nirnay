20 Real-Life Dilemmas. 20 Opportunities to Think Better.

Practise thoughtful decision-making by exploring a real-life situation, weighing options and explaining your choice.

Overview

Each 90-minute experience is standalone. You will examine assumptions, weigh trade-offs and consider consequences before deciding.

Example Dilemmas

Investment Crisis

A ₹2 crore portfolio falls to ₹1.30 crore. Your father says hold, a friend says sell and your adviser says buy more. What would you do?

Family Business

Your family offers you a ₹100 crore business, but your interests lie elsewhere. Which path will you take?

Wealth vs Purpose

At 25, you inherit ₹20 crore. What gives your life purpose when work is no longer financially necessary?
